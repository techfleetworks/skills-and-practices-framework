# Onboarding Project Training Teams with Agile Coaches

During the three weeks of project training onboarding, Agile Coaches who are also training in the community lead workshops and activities. They help teams transform their mindsets and behaviors to the Agile Ways of Life, [living the agile philosophies](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-philosophies/agile-ways-of-work.md) and building [self-actualized agile team practices.](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams.md)
