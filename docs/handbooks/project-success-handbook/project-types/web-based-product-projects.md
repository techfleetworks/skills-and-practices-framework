# Web-Based Product Projects

## What Kind of Work Does It Entail?&#x20;

During a web-based product project, a team gets together to produce a digital web-based experience. This could be an information website, a platform, or something else. The specific outcome to be achieved is always based on the project goals, but the [agile lifecycle](/project-portal/project-success-handbook/project-components/agile-project-lifecycle.md) determines the steps in the process that teams go through to solve the right problems and solve the problems in the right ways. The end result of this project is a digital tool for the world to use in an end-to-end experience.
