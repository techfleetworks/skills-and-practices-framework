# Key Traits We Look For in Team Mates

## Key Traits of Successful Teammates

1. [Agile philosophy](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-philosophies.md) mindset
2. [Service Leader](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/self-actualized-agile-teams/service-leadership.md) attitude
3. No ego
4. Here to grow together, not to just produce work
5. Willingness to [fail and learn ](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/prioritizing-growth-over-skills.md)in the Agile way
6. Willingness to be a [cross-functional team mate](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/day-to-day-on-an-agile-team/cross-functional-agile-teamwork.md)
7. Aligned with [Tech Fleet’s values](https://guide.techfleet.org/about-us/about-our-org/mission-and-values#our-values) and mission
8. High passion for giving back to the nonprofits we work with
