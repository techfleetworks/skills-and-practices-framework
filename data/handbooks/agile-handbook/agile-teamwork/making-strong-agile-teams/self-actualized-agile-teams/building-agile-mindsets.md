## Agile Team Strength is Continuously Maintained

Team strength is not measured in how much work they can get done. In Agile environments it's measured in agility. Teams should build strong foundations of celebrating team mates, supporting growth, taking risks, and experimenting with new ways of working to successfully deliver strong results.&#x20;

Agility changes over time, and it doesn't happen all at once. This is because it requires a [huge mindset shift for teammates](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/start-the-agile-handbook-here/introduction-to-the-agile-handbook.md).&#x20;

The philosophies of Agile must be constantly maintained, just like a garden must be watered and upkept.&#x20;

Team members check in with others and everyone acts in support of everyone's growth.&#x20;

## Foundations of Strong Agile Teams

Everyone on an Agile team leads in service to others: the interns, the junior contributors, the managers, and the executives.&#x20;

At its core, all Agile teams who are strongly performing have the following principles built among the team within their culture:&#x20;

1. [Psychological Safety](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/self-actualized-agile-teams/psychological-safety.md)
2. [Service Leadership](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/self-actualized-agile-teams/service-leadership.md)
3. [Self-Organization](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/self-actualized-agile-teams/self-organization.md)
4. [Continuous Improvement](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/self-actualized-agile-teams/continuous-improvement.md)
5. [Iterative Value Delivery](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/self-actualized-agile-teams/iterative-value-delivery.md)

There is a Pyramid of Agile Tenets that makes self-actualized Agile teams.&#x20;

This picture shows the hierarchy of Agile team needs and what must be in place before teams can [Perform](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/the-four-stages-of-team-growth.md).&#x20;

Without a foundation of psychological safety and service leadership, teams cannot become strong Agile teams. They will [Storm ](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/the-four-stages-of-team-growth.md)longer. They may storm forever, and never get to [Performing](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/the-four-stages-of-team-growth.md). They won't get as much work done either!&#x20;
