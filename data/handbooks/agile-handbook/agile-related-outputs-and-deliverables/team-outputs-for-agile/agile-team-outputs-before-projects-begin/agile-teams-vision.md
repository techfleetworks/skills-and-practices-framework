## What is Agile Team Vision?

Just like a product has a vision, a team needs a vision too. Vision helps everyone see long-term outcomes and direction ahead. Teams can build a collective vision for themselves as teammates before projects begin to outline:&#x20;

1. The audiences they serve
2. The team-focused problems to solve
3. The team goals
4. The value the team provides
5. What makes the team unique
6. What risks the team can identify to vet
7. The costs that may go into the team
8. The scope of Agile coaching and transformation work ahead
9. How the team measures success

### The team-focused problems to solve

### The team goals

### The value the team provides

When you solve the problems you’ve identified for your team, which pieces of value will your team deliver to the world? Create one value point per problem statement.

<img src="/files/ZD9vVsZZXYvtvJk1BZ3i" alt="Showing the value of a team for a team vision. Credit: Tech Fleet." data-size="original">

### What makes the team unique

### What risks the team can identify to mitigate

What are the things that could stand in the way of your Agile Transformation succeeding in the long-term? List anything related to team user experience, technology, market, business, or otherwise. Once you identify these risks, your team should work toward removing risks.

## Why is it Valuable?

Team vision boards are valuable because they give every team member a stake in the outcomes from the beginning of work together. The vision helps a team understand why they are here together and what they are working towards.&#x20;

## How is it Made?

A team should produce this together in collaboration.&#x20;

## Who Maintains Agile Team Vision?

The [Scrum Master](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-methods/scrum-method/scrum-team-functions.md) facilitates and maintains the Agile Team Vision on the team.&#x20;

## When Should Teams Build their Agile Vision?

Teams make it before a project, and update it after the project begins as they change their working agreements.&#x20;

## Key Components of Building an Agile Team Vision

1. **Team vision boards represent the voice of the team:** A [self-organized ](/agile-training-portal/agile-handbook/agile-teamwork/making-strong-agile-teams/self-actualized-agile-teams/self-organization.md)team makes their own decisions and determines their own future together. No one person should tell them why they are here, or what they are working towards. It takes an entire team to provide voices and perspectives into their mission and purpose together.&#x20;
