# Timeline for Agile Outputs

## When to Deliver Agile Outputs

Self-organized teams need to make their own decisions about whether and when to deliver Agile-focused outputs. Here are the recommended sequences of delivering these things:

1. Generating Agile buy-in
2. Agile team vision
3. Team RACI
4. Working agreements
5. Project phase roadmap
6. Sprint planning items
7. Sprint retro
8. Agile maturity measurements
