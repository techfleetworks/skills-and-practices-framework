## Creating the User Experience

The user experience could include content, screens, interaction design, or layouts of information. For service design projects, teams should design the services that will be launched themselves. Teams should decide what work to include together.

## Continuing Research

Research never stops on an agile team. [Continuous discovery](/project-portal/project-success-handbook/project-components/project-milestones/continuous-discovery-milestone.md) is called "continuous" for a reason! Teams always have new design decisions to validate, new assumptions to test, new ideas to test.
