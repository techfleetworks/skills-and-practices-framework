# Contributing to the Skills & Practices Framework

Thank you for helping improve an open framework for how people work well together.

## How the data is maintained

- The **source of truth for the data** is authored in Baserow and exported to this
  repo with `tools/sync-from-baserow.mjs`. Do not hand-edit files in `data/` — they
  are generated. Propose data changes through an issue or a discussion so they can be
  made at the source and re-exported.
- The **docs** (`README.md`, `docs/data-types/`, `docs/handbooks/`) and **tooling**
  (`tools/`, `schema/`) can be edited directly via pull request.

## Proposing a change

1. Open an issue describing the change and why it improves the framework.
2. For new categories or structural changes, expect a short review discussion, since
   this framework aims to be a shared standard.
3. Keep the plain-language, 8th-grade voice used across the docs.

## Ground rules

- Be specific and cite sources where you can.
- Keep personal or individual assessment data out of this public repo. The taxonomy
  is public; anyone's private answers or scores are not.
